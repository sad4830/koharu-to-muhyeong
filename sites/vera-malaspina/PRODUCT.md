# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Vinext / Next.js 16 / React 19 / Tailwind CSS 4 starter selected by the permanent character-site factory.

## Users

- WACA roleplay participants who need to understand Vera Malaspina quickly before interacting with her.
- WACA administrators who need to verify required profile fields, Grade II limits, ability counters, penalties, and world-setting compliance.
- The character owner, who needs a durable mobile-friendly reference during roleplay.

## Product Purpose

Present Vera Malaspina's complete WACA character profile as a single long-form web experience. The surface must support a twenty-second identity read, a three-minute causal summary, and a deep reference path for abilities, backstory, current conflict, and open relationship hooks.

Success means no confirmed Notion field is omitted, ability rules remain easy to retrieve during roleplay, and Vera's chaotic personality is felt without reducing legibility.

## Positioning

This is not a generic character-card gallery. It is a readable experimental dossier whose structure becomes less controlled as Vera's self-justifying story fractures, while every factual rule remains stable and auditable.

## Operating Context

- Used as an external character-profile link for WACA review and roleplay reference.
- Expected to be read frequently on a tall mobile screen as well as desktop.
- One public route contains the profile. Long sections may use accessible disclosures and anchored navigation.
- The user-provided AI image is visual reference only. It must not be embedded, uploaded, or used as the character seal.
- Appearance is represented through the approved text description.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the complete confirmed Notion profile, including identity, text-only appearance, personality, traits, affiliation, Grade II abilities, costs, counters, backstory, three-layer truth, current project, Lust interpretation, relic and authority status, preferences, relationship hooks, timeline, and dialogue.
- Preserve WACA form terminology: XX, II grade, Septem Peccata, Lust sinner, two abilities, no relic, unawakened authority.
- Keep all ability quantities, ranges, durations, cooldowns, total limits, and counters unchanged.
- Keep projectile speed explicit: 25 m/s for the glass ampoule and 20 m/s for the glass flask, both measured immediately after release with no supernatural acceleration or homing.
- Do not add firearms, extra powers, relic effects, non-human species traits, or approved outcomes for other owners' characters.
- Do not place the AI reference image or any generated character likeness in the site.
- No factual commercial claims, testimonials, analytics claims, or invented WACA approvals.

## Brand Commitments

- Character name: Vera Malaspina / 베라 말라스피나.
- Activity and sin name: Errata / 에라타.
- Core voice: fast, contradictory, profane when excited, delighted by failed experiments, dangerous rather than merely comedic. Every in-character line uses casual Korean speech regardless of age, rank, affiliation, or situation.
- Recurring narrative materials: fluorescent green reagent, mislabelled experiments, the closed isolation door, and knocking that returns in silence.
- The site must feel volatile and asymmetrical while the information hierarchy remains dependable.

## Evidence on Hand

- Canonical Notion page: `3bdff93a-fd1e-81b6-a462-f30c478b0138`.
- Official WACA profile form, character inspection guide, world entrance, and general notice supplied by the user.
- User-supplied appearance reference image, excluded from the shipped site and used only to author the text appearance description.
- No approved character illustration, logo, testimonial, or additional media asset is available.

## Product Principles

1. Canon parity before visual compression.
2. Ability mechanics are discoverable in seconds and never hidden behind motion.
3. Chaos belongs to composition, language, and interaction, not broken navigation.
4. Public facts, deeper truths, and open relationship hooks remain clearly distinguished.
5. Mobile reading and reduced-motion access are first-class, not fallbacks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard, touch, and screen-reader users must reach the same content.
- Motion honors `prefers-reduced-motion` and content remains visible without animation.
- Text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Focus states remain visible, and no meaning relies only on fluorescent green color.
