# WACA: Karma Archive

## Product truth

- This is a single-character WACA profile and narrative experience for one original adult female character.
- The character must be human, XX, at least 20 years old, WACA-aligned, and classified as II grade.
- II grade allows at most two abilities, including any relic ability. The ceiling is about 2 GJ, an 80 m effective radius, and limited-area suppression. Her kit must stay inside that ceiling.
- Every ability and relic entry must explain effect, range, duration, cooldown, penalty, and practical counterplay.
- The setting year is 2027. Ordinary roleplay is centered in Bethlehem Special Sanctuary.
- WACA knows spiritual energy and supernatural crime, maintains secrecy, and uses ARK devices for emergency response, recording, location, translation, and civilian memory adjustment.
- The character belongs beneath one Apostle as a disciple. She is not a high-ranking or semi-high-ranking character and has no pre-awakened Authority.
- The profile must include the official form fields: name, age, biological sex, nationality, height and weight, written appearance, personality, traits, affiliation, department and rank, branch, grade, abilities, relic if used, likes and dislikes, and notes.
- No character portrait, generated exterior image, silhouette, MV still, or copied illustration will be used. Appearance is described in text only.

## User intent

- This release is meant to be a masterpiece, with story and visual design treated as equal priorities.
- The public profile should already feel complete and emotionally satisfying.
- A sealed record should then reframe earlier scenes, making the reader reconsider the character's guilt, lost relationship, and self-judgment.
- The abilities must emerge from the character's decisive past choice. Combat behavior, limitations, and cost must continue that same story rather than decorate it.
- The site should feel like opening a sealed WACA judgment record, not like browsing a generic character card page.

## Theme interpretation

- Dareharu's Karma is used as the first and only theme song through the official YouTube video, ID `jv543Nk5s18`.
- Entering the actual dossier requires an explicit gate action so browsers can legally begin audible playback from 0:00. The full official song then plays once; the player remains visible and controllable.
- The narrative translates the song through loss, memory, masks, split identity, self-judgment, and the return of one's own actions.
- The final movement must not reduce the song to literal death. It follows the officially revealed hidden chorus `피어나` and resolves toward the terror and desire of living as one's unborrowed self.

## Audience and experience

- Audience: Korean character roleplayers who value setting compliance, narrative hooks, balanced abilities, and emotionally coherent profiles.
- Visitor scene: a quiet, dark room where the reader opens a classified record and listens to the song while reading.
- First viewport proof: a broken time, one remaining breath, II-grade classification, the character's verdict line, and the action that opens both the record and the official song.
- Primary path: enter record, understand the operative, inspect her relic and exact combat rules, follow the incident chronology, unlock the sealed testimony, and reach a playable present-day decision.

## Constraints

- Preserve WACA's current source rules and labels. Do not present invented details as official setting facts.
- Keep all copy in Korean except established acronyms, document codes, and the song's official English title.
- Avoid em dash and en dash characters in all visible copy.
- No audio extraction, unauthorized audio hosting, or hidden player. Use the official YouTube embed and an explicit fallback link.
- Mobile and desktop must both be first-class. Respect reduced-motion and keyboard navigation.
- No generic same-size card grid, gradient text, decorative technical monospace, or ornamental glass effects.
