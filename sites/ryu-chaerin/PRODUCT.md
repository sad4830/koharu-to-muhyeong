# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Sites Vinext starter. The requested deployment also needs a self-contained Next.js-compatible factory copy for Vercel.

## Users

- The primary reader is a WACA roleplay participant checking whether an original character is clear, usable, and compliant with the community profile and balance rules.
- A secondary reader is a roleplay partner who needs to understand the character's personality, support abilities, limits, and counterplay at a glance during a scene.

## Product Purpose

Present one original WACA character as a polished, readable profile site. Success means a first-time reader can identify the character, affiliation, rank, role, and both abilities immediately, then retrieve detailed rules without ambiguity.

## Positioning

The profile translates a professional cheerleader's timing, formation control, and morale into two practical support abilities while keeping every effect measurable in roleplay posts.

## Operating Context

- Canon setting year: 2027.
- Organization: World Ability Crime Alliance (WACA), headquartered in the Bethlehem Special Sanctuary with eleven national branches.
- WACA members use the ARK watch for rescue signals, reports, recording, location sharing, emergency communication, translation, and civilian memory adjustment.
- The profile follows the official Postype profile form, inspection guide, full-world setting, and general notice supplied by the user.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Original adult human woman, age 21, Korean, WACA member, Grade II.
- Professional cheerleader, described as a notably beautiful woman with an athletic and well-proportioned figure.
- Exactly two abilities. Both are support abilities, not direct attacks.
- Each ability must state activation, range, targets, duration in roleplay posts, cooldown in roleplay posts, penalties, limitations, and counterplay in plain Korean.
- Grade II benchmark: up to two abilities and an influence range no greater than 80 metres.
- No MJ or GJ notation in visible copy.
- No firearms.
- Do not create, generate, or imply a character portrait. The site may use typography, geometry, icons, and motion only.
- Preserve canonical Korean terminology and distinguish original character lore from confirmed setting facts.

## Brand Commitments

- Site identity: a WACA operational record interpreted through professional cheerleading, arena formations, scoreboards, and rally rhythm.
- Character page, not a generic business landing page.
- One strong accent family and a dark/light system-preference theme.
- Taste Skill, Impeccable, and commercial-site quality standards are explicitly required.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official Postype pages supplied by the user: profile form, character inspection guide, world-setting entry/full WACA world page, and general notice.
- No licensed or user-provided character image exists. Future work must not fabricate one or add a placeholder portrait.
- Character biography and abilities are original draft material and must not be presented as official WACA canon.

## Product Principles

1. Rules are readable before they are decorative.
2. Every support effect has a cost and a clear way to break or avoid it.
3. Cheerleading influences interaction, movement, rhythm, and voice rather than becoming a cosmetic label.
4. The interface feels energetic without obstructing long-form reading or reduced-motion users.
5. No visual asset implies an appearance source that does not exist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-complete navigation and disclosures, visible focus, 44px minimum touch targets, logical headings, reduced-motion support, and readable Korean line lengths from 320px through wide desktop.
